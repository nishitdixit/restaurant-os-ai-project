# Restaurant OS — Explore the Demo

**Your own restaurant website + direct ordering + restaurant operations, all in one place.**

Explore Restaurant OS as if **Saffron Street Kitchen** were your restaurant. See the experience your customers get, then
switch to the restaurant dashboard to see how the business is run.

## 🍽️ Customer Website

https://restaurant-os-ai-project.vercel.app

What your customers see: they browse the menu, customise dishes, order direct and track their order. Best on a phone.

## ⚙️ Restaurant Admin

https://restaurant-os-ai-project-admin.vercel.app

Where you run the restaurant: orders, menu, offers, customers, analytics and your website's look. Best on a laptop or
tablet.

1. Browse the customer website.
2. Add a dish with your choice of options and place a test order.
3. Open **Admin → Orders** and manage your order.
4. Open **Appearance** and change the website design.
5. Explore **Menu**, **Offers**, **Customers** and **Analytics**.

## ⚡ Demo performance note

> **About demo speed:** This is a showcase environment on low-cost development hosting, not the production setup.
> When it hasn't been used for a while, it goes to sleep, so the first page can take a little longer to load. Once it's
> awake, everything responds quickly. A restaurant's live setup runs on production-grade hosting and doesn't have this
> delay.
