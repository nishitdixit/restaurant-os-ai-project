# Research Notes / External Constraints

## Google discovery

The product may improve technical discoverability through crawlable pages, structured data, accurate business information, good performance and merchant configuration workflows. Search/local ranking remains controlled by external ranking systems; never promise a specific position.

## Third-party ordering/delivery platforms

Do not scrape, reverse engineer or impersonate marketplace interfaces. Integrate through official APIs/partner programs where available and commercially permitted.

## Regulatory / compliance note

Before production launch, obtain current professional guidance for Indian GST/tax treatment, payments, privacy/data handling, WhatsApp/SMS communication rules, consumer protection requirements and any sector-specific obligations.

This file records product constraints, not legal advice.
