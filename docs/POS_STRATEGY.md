# POS Strategy

## Product decision

Do not build a full hardware POS in MVP.

The platform should remain POS-compatible at the domain level by treating orders as channel-neutral.

## Future POS direction

If market demand exists, support:

- counter orders;
- cash/card/UPI;
- receipt printing;
- barcode/scanner integrations where relevant;
- kitchen routing;
- end-of-day closure.

## Why defer

Hardware, printer compatibility, offline mode and cash management can multiply support complexity. Prove digital/direct delivery workflows first.
