# Privacy and Data Handling

## Data categories

Potential personal data includes:

- name;
- phone;
- email;
- delivery address;
- order history;
- communication preferences.

## Principles

- collect only what the workflow needs;
- define purpose for marketing use;
- isolate tenant data;
- minimize PII in logs;
- restrict exports;
- define retention/deletion policy;
- secure credentials and secrets.

## Customer-facing controls

Where appropriate, provide access to terms/privacy notice, communication preferences and support contact.

## Merchant controls

Merchant should be able to manage marketing consent/preferences consistent with supported communication channels and applicable laws.

## Compliance

Before production commercialization, obtain current legal/compliance review for Indian privacy, consumer, tax, payment and marketing requirements. This document is an engineering baseline, not legal advice.
