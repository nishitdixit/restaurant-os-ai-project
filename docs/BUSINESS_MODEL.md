# Business Model

## Positioning

Subscription SaaS for independent food businesses.

## Likely monetization layers

- base monthly subscription;
- additional outlet/brand fees;
- premium domain/theme features;
- messaging usage;
- advanced analytics/AI;
- delivery/integration usage where commercially justified;
- white-label/partner plans later.

## Pricing philosophy

Price against business value and operational savings, not feature count alone.

## Unit economics to monitor

- MRR per tenant;
- gross margin;
- infrastructure cost per active tenant;
- messaging/delivery/payment pass-through costs;
- support cost per tenant;
- tenant churn;
- activation time;
- direct order volume.

## Sales wedge

Initial sale should be easy:

“Get your own direct-order website and restaurant operations dashboard.”

Long-term value expansion:

“Run more of your business from one system.”

## Avoid

A zero-margin business where the platform earns only from low-value payment or delivery pass-through without recurring SaaS revenue.
