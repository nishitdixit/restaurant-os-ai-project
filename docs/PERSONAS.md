# User Personas

## 1. Owner-operator

Runs the business, often handles marketing and operational decisions personally.

Needs speed, visibility and low cognitive load.

Success looks like fewer manual tasks, more direct orders and clearer margins.

## 2. Kitchen manager

Responsible for order intake and production flow.

Needs an unambiguous queue, stock visibility and simple status actions.

## 3. Staff/admin

Performs repetitive updates and order operations.

Needs permissions appropriate to role and limited blast radius.

## 4. Customer

Often on mobile and in a hurry.

Needs fast discovery, trustworthy information, clear prices, simple checkout and predictable fulfillment.

## 5. Platform administrator

Manages tenants, subscriptions, configuration, support and platform health.

Needs tenant-safe support tools and strong auditability.

## Persona principle

Never optimize a workflow for the internal admin if that makes the customer experience materially worse, and never optimize a customer feature by creating hidden operational work.
