# SEO and Local Discovery Strategy

## Goal

Maximize the merchant's technical discoverability for branded and relevant local intent without making ranking guarantees.

## Public SEO requirements

- server-rendered/indexable content pages;
- semantic HTML;
- stable clean URLs;
- descriptive titles/descriptions;
- canonical URLs;
- sitemap generation;
- robots configuration;
- internal linking;
- optimized images with dimensions/alt text;
- structured data where supported and accurate;
- fast page delivery;
- mobile usability;
- unique product/category content;
- no accidental noindex on commercial pages.

## Storefront URL strategy

Example:

`/{category}`

`/menu/{product-slug}`

`/offers/{offer-slug}`

`/locations/{area}` only when the content is genuinely useful and distinct; avoid mass-generated thin pages.

## Business profile support

The product should give merchants a setup checklist for their Google Business Profile, menu URL and ordering URL. Where supported by official APIs/programs, integrations may automate portions of this workflow.

## Structured data

Use appropriate schema.org types such as Restaurant/LocalBusiness and Product where semantically valid. Keep data accurate and synchronized with the visible page.

## Local landing pages

Create only real, useful location/service-area pages. Do not generate hundreds of near-duplicate pages solely to capture keywords.

## Search Console

Provide setup instructions and later optional integration for health/performance insights.

## SEO health score

A simple merchant-facing checklist can score:

- profile completeness;
- title/meta completeness;
- sitemap status;
- indexability;
- broken links;
- image health;
- structured-data presence;
- performance signals;
- business profile/order-link configuration.

The score measures readiness, not guaranteed ranking.

## Anti-patterns

- keyword stuffing;
- hidden text;
- fake location pages;
- copied menu descriptions across tenants;
- misleading structured data;
- promises of guaranteed position.
