# Storefront Spec

## Goal

Create a branded, fast, mobile-first customer storefront.

## Requirements

- category navigation;
- product pages;
- search;
- product availability;
- add-to-cart;
- sticky/direct order CTA patterns appropriate to device;
- SEO metadata;
- responsive images;
- accessible navigation.

## Acceptance

A new visitor can reach checkout from a product in a small number of actions and all customer-visible prices come from the server-authoritative catalog state.
