# V1 Direction

## Objective

Turn the pilot into a repeatable product used by multiple paying restaurants.

## Key additions

- self-serve onboarding;
- custom domains;
- theme editor — delivered early in the MVP (ADR-012: per-restaurant themes, 12 presets);
- richer analytics;
- customer segments;
- WhatsApp campaigns;
- order/kitchen display improvements;
- reordering;
- referral/loyalty basics;
- delivery provider abstraction in production;
- subscription billing;
- platform admin;
- operational reporting;
- support tooling.

## V1 success signals

- multiple active tenants;
- low merchant support burden;
- high direct-order completion rate;
- repeat usage of admin workflows;
- clear willingness to pay;
- predictable deployment and onboarding process.
