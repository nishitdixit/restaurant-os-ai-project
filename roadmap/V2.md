# V2 Direction

## Objective

Move from digital storefront + basic operations toward a deeper restaurant operating system.

## Candidate modules

- ingredient inventory;
- recipe/BOM;
- stock ledger;
- waste tracking;
- supplier/procurement basics;
- multi-outlet;
- multi-brand;
- shared kitchen inventory;
- aggregator synchronization where supported;
- automated delivery booking;
- advanced customer journeys;
- contribution margin reporting;
- experiments and offer optimization.
